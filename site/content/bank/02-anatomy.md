## Joints, bones and levers
```written
Level: Analyse
Q: Explain how the skeleton, joints and muscles work together when a footballer kicks a ball.
* The quadriceps contract (agonist) and the hamstrings relax (antagonist) :: quadricep* & (agonist|contract*|extend*) | hamstring* & (antagonist|relax*) :: Which muscle straightens the knee and which one has to relax to allow it?
* Muscles pull on bones through tendons (the insertion moves towards the origin) :: tendon* | insertion | origin | pull* :: How does a contracting muscle move a bone?
* The knee is a hinge joint that moves through extension :: hinge | extension | extend* | straighten* :: Which type of joint is the knee and what movement does it make in a kick?
* Bones act as levers :: lever* :: What role do the long bones of the leg play when muscles pull on them?
* Cartilage and synovial fluid reduce friction so the joint moves smoothly :: cartilage | synovial fluid | friction | lubricat* :: Which joint features help the bones move smoothly?
Model: When a footballer kicks, the quadriceps contract as the agonist while the hamstrings relax as the antagonist. The quadriceps pull on the tibia through a tendon, so the insertion moves towards the origin. The knee is a hinge joint and extends to straighten the leg, and the leg bones act as levers. Cartilage and synovial fluid reduce friction so the joint moves smoothly.
```

```written
Q: Compare a ball and socket joint with a hinge joint. Explain why each suits its job, using examples.
* Ball and socket allows movement in all directions and rotation (for example shoulder or hip) :: ball & socket & (all directions|rotation|rotate*|wide|greatest) | shoulder | hip :: What range of movement does a ball and socket joint allow?
* Hinge only bends and straightens in one direction (for example knee or elbow) :: hinge & (one direction|bend*|straighten*|flex*|extend*) | knee | elbow :: What movements can a hinge joint make?
* The shoulder needs a wide range to throw, swim or reach :: throw* | swim* | reach* | bowl* | wide range :: Why does a shoulder need a wide range of movement?

Model: A ball and socket joint such as the shoulder or hip allows movement in all directions and rotation, so the shoulder has a wide range to throw, swim and reach. A hinge joint such as the knee or elbow only bends and straightens in one direction, which makes the knee more stable to support body weight and push off. However, a joint with more movement is less stable, so the shoulder has a greater risk of dislocation.
```
