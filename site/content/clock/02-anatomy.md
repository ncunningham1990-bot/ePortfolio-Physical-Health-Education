```quiz
Q: Which is a function of the skeleton?
- Digesting food | That is the job of the digestive system.
- Producing hormones | Hormones are made by glands.
+ Protecting vital organs
- Pumping blood | The heart does that, not the skeleton.

Q: Which bone protects the brain?
- Sternum | The sternum protects organs in the chest.
+ Cranium
- Femur | The femur is in the upper leg.
- Pelvis | The pelvis protects organs in the lower body.

Q: Which bone is commonly called the shin bone?
- Fibula | The fibula is the thinner bone beside it.
+ Tibia
- Femur | The femur is above the knee.
- Humerus | The humerus is in the upper arm.

Q: Which type of bone is the femur?
- Flat | Flat bones are thin and protective, like the cranium.
- Short | Short bones are small and roughly cube-shaped.
+ Long
- Irregular | Irregular bones have unusual shapes, like the vertebrae.

Q: The knee and elbow are examples of which type of synovial joint?
- Ball and socket | That joint allows movement in many directions.
+ Hinge
- Pivot | A pivot joint allows rotation, such as turning the head.
- Gliding | Gliding joints slide slightly, like those in the wrist.

Q: The hip and shoulder are examples of which type of synovial joint?
- Hinge | A hinge only bends and straightens.
+ Ball and socket
- Pivot | A pivot allows rotation only around one axis.
- Condyloid | That joint allows movement in two planes only.

Q: What does synovial fluid do inside a joint?
- Joins bone to muscle | Tendons do that job.
+ Lubricates the joint and reduces friction
- Joins bone to bone | Ligaments do that job.
- Makes red blood cells | Blood cells are made inside bones, not the fluid.

Q: Which structure connects bone to bone?
- Tendon | A tendon connects muscle to bone.
+ Ligament
- Cartilage | Cartilage covers bone ends and absorbs shock.
- Synovial membrane | That membrane makes the synovial fluid.

Q: Which structure connects muscle to bone?
- Ligament | A ligament connects bone to bone.
+ Tendon
- Cartilage | Cartilage covers the ends of bones.
- Joint capsule | The capsule surrounds the joint.

Q: What is the movement that decreases the angle at a joint?
- Extension | Extension does the opposite.
+ Flexion
- Abduction | Abduction moves a limb away from the body.
- Rotation | Rotation is a turning movement.

Q: Moving an arm out sideways away from the body is called what?
- Adduction | Adduction moves the limb towards the body.
- Flexion | Flexion bends the joint rather than moving the limb sideways.
+ Abduction
- Circumduction | Circumduction is a circular movement.

Q: Which type of muscle is under voluntary control?
- Cardiac | Cardiac muscle works automatically in the heart.
- Smooth | Smooth muscle works without you thinking about it.
+ Skeletal
- Involuntary | That word describes how cardiac and smooth muscle work, so it is not a muscle type.

Q: Which muscle is at the front of the upper leg?
- Hamstrings | The hamstrings are at the back of the thigh.
+ Quadriceps
- Gastrocnemius | That muscle is in the calf.
- Deltoid | The deltoid is in the shoulder.

Q: Which muscle is the main muscle on the front of the upper arm?
- Triceps | The triceps are on the back of the upper arm.
+ Biceps
- Latissimus dorsi | That is a large muscle of the back.
- Trapezius | That muscle is across the upper back and neck.

Q: At the elbow, which muscle is the antagonist when the biceps contract to bend the arm?
- Deltoid | The deltoid works mostly at the shoulder.
- Biceps | The biceps is the muscle doing the work, so look at its opposite.
+ Triceps
- Pectorals | The pectorals are on the chest.

Q: What is the muscle that produces the movement called?
- Antagonist | The antagonist relaxes to allow the movement.
- Fixator | A fixator stabilises a joint but does not drive the movement.
+ Agonist
- Synergist | A synergist assists the agonist but is not the main mover.

Q: Which muscle fibre type is best for a 100 m sprint?
- Slow twitch | Slow twitch fibres resist fatigue but contract slowly.
+ Fast twitch
- Cardiac | That is a muscle type, not a fibre type.
- Smooth | That is a muscle type, not a fibre type.

Q: Which type of fibre is best suited to a marathon?
- Fast twitch | Fast twitch fibres tire quickly.
+ Slow twitch
- Type 2b | This type produces power quickly but fatigues fast.
- Cardiac | That is a muscle type, not a fibre type.

Q: What type of muscle contraction happens when a muscle shortens as it produces force?
- Eccentric | In an eccentric contraction the muscle lengthens.
- Isometric | In an isometric contraction the muscle stays the same length.
+ Concentric
- Isotonic | That is a general word for contractions with movement, but it does not name the shortening type.

Q: Holding a plank is an example of which type of muscle contraction?
- Concentric | A concentric contraction needs the muscle to shorten.
- Eccentric | An eccentric contraction needs the muscle to lengthen.
+ Isometric
- Isokinetic | That describes movement at a constant speed.
```
