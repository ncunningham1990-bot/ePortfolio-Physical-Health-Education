```quiz
Q: Which nutrient is the body's main source of energy?
- Protein | Protein's main job is growth and repair.
- Vitamins | Vitamins help the body work, but they do not provide energy.
+ Carbohydrate
- Water | Water is vital but does not provide energy.

Q: Which nutrient is mainly used for growth and repair of muscle?
- Carbohydrate | Carbohydrate is mainly for energy.
+ Protein
- Fibre | Fibre helps digestion.
- Vitamins | Vitamins help the body work, but they do not build muscle.

Q: Which food is a good source of protein?
- White rice | Rice is mostly carbohydrate.
+ Chicken
- Butter | Butter is mostly fat.
- Apple | An apple is mostly carbohydrate and water.

Q: Which food is a good source of carbohydrate?
- Egg | Eggs are mainly protein and fat.
+ Pasta
- Cheese | Cheese is mostly protein and fat.
- Olive oil | Oil is almost entirely fat.

Q: What is the role of fat in the diet?
- Quick energy for sprinting | Carbohydrate is the quicker fuel for hard efforts.
+ A concentrated energy store and helps absorb some vitamins
- Building muscle fibres | Protein is the main nutrient for that.
- Cleaning the bloodstream | That is not a role of fat.

Q: Which kind of fat is healthier?
- Saturated | Saturated fat in large amounts is linked to heart problems.
- Trans | Trans fats are the least healthy kind.
+ Unsaturated
- Hydrogenated | This is a process that often creates unhealthy fats.

Q: Which food is a good source of unsaturated fat?
- Fatty bacon | Bacon is high in saturated fat.
+ Avocado
- Butter | Butter is high in saturated fat.
- Cream | Cream is high in saturated fat.

Q: What is the main job of fibre?
- To give energy | Fibre passes through the body without being used for energy.
+ To keep the digestive system healthy
- To build muscle | That is the job of protein.
- To carry oxygen | That is the job of red blood cells and iron.

Q: Which mineral is needed to make haemoglobin in red blood cells?
- Calcium | Calcium is needed for bones and teeth.
+ Iron
- Sodium | Sodium helps with fluid balance and nerves.
- Potassium | Potassium helps muscles and nerves work.

Q: Which mineral helps build strong bones and teeth?
- Iron | Iron helps carry oxygen.
+ Calcium
- Sodium | Sodium helps with fluid balance.
- Zinc | Zinc supports healing and the immune system.

Q: Which vitamin helps the body absorb calcium?
- Vitamin C | Vitamin C is mostly linked to healing and the immune system.
- Vitamin B12 | This vitamin supports blood and nerves.
+ Vitamin D
- Vitamin K | This vitamin helps blood to clot.

Q: Which is a good source of vitamin C?
- Bread | Bread is mainly carbohydrate.
+ Oranges
- Cheese | Cheese is mainly protein and fat.
- Rice | Rice is mainly carbohydrate.

Q: Why do athletes need to stay hydrated?
- Water gives them energy | Water does not provide energy.
+ Water helps control body temperature and carries nutrients
- Water builds muscle | Muscles are built from protein.
- Water replaces carbohydrate | Water cannot replace a nutrient.

Q: Which is an early sign of dehydration?
- A very pale urine | Pale urine usually means you are well hydrated.
+ A headache and dark urine
- A lower heart rate | Dehydration tends to raise the heart rate.
- Feeling very energetic | Fatigue is much more typical.

Q: What does a balanced diet include?
- Only protein | A balanced diet needs a range of nutrients.
- No fat at all | The body needs some fat to work properly.
+ The right proportions of all the food groups
- Only fruit and vegetables | These are healthy, but they do not provide everything needed.

Q: What does BMR stand for?
- Basic muscle rate | That is not an energy term.
+ Basal metabolic rate
- Body mass ratio | That sounds close, but it is a different measure.
- Blood movement rate | That is not an energy term.

Q: What is BMR?
- The energy used in a hard workout | That describes energy used in activity.
+ The energy the body needs at rest to stay alive
- The energy found in food | That describes the energy intake, not what the body uses.
- The energy lost in sweat | Sweat loses water and salts, not an energy measure.

Q: Which unit is commonly used to measure the energy in food?
- Newtons | Newtons measure force.
+ Kilocalories
- Litres | Litres measure volume.
- Watts | Watts measure power.

Q: If energy intake is lower than energy expenditure, what will usually happen to body weight?
- It will go up | Think about what happens when you use more than you take in.
- It will stay exactly the same | Energy balance only holds when the two are equal.
+ It will go down
- It will double | That is not realistic for an energy imbalance.
```
