```quiz
Q: Which of these is part of the definition of health used in the course?
- Only being free from illness | The definition goes beyond that.
+ Physical, mental and social well-being
- Being able to run fast | Being fast is one fitness quality and not the whole definition.
- Having a lot of muscle | Muscle size is not what health means.

Q: What does PAR-Q stand for?
- Personal Activity Report Questionnaire | Close, but the first word is different.
+ Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire
- Performance And Recovery Quiz | That is not the real name.
- Physical Ability Rating Quiz | That is not the real name.

Q: What is the purpose of a PAR-Q?
- To test your fitness | Fitness tests are separate from the questionnaire.
+ To check it is safe for you to start exercising
- To measure your BMI | That is done with height and weight measurements.
- To choose your sport | The PAR-Q is about safety, not choosing a sport.

Q: In RICE, what does the C stand for?
- Cooling | Cooling is not one of the RICE steps.
+ Compression
- Care | That is not one of the steps.
- Cardio | Cardio exercise is not part of first aid.

Q: What is the main reason for using ice on an injury?
- To warm the area | Ice has the opposite effect.
+ To reduce swelling and pain
- To stretch the muscle | Ice does not stretch muscle.
- To speed up movement | Ice is used to settle the injury, not to move it.

Q: Which body type is tall, thin and lightly muscled?
- Mesomorph | A mesomorph has a muscular, athletic build.
- Endomorph | An endomorph is rounder with more body fat.
+ Ectomorph
- Somatotype | That is the name of the classification system, not a body type.

Q: Which body type is muscular with a wide chest and shoulders?
- Ectomorph | An ectomorph is lean and light.
- Endomorph | An endomorph carries more fat.
+ Mesomorph
- Pyknic | That is not one of the three somatotypes.

Q: Which type of motivation comes from inside, such as enjoying the activity?
- Extrinsic | Extrinsic motivation comes from outside rewards.
+ Intrinsic
- Aggressive | That is not a motivation type.
- Social | That describes a source of support, not a type of motivation.

Q: Winning a trophy is an example of which type of motivation?
- Intrinsic | Intrinsic comes from within the person.
+ Extrinsic
- Internal | That is not the name of the motivation type.
- Arousal | Arousal is about alertness, not motivation.

Q: What does arousal describe?
- The score in a game | That is an outcome, not arousal.
+ How alert and ready your body and mind are
- How well you slept | Sleep affects arousal, but it is not what arousal means.
- Your fitness level | Fitness is different from arousal.

Q: According to the inverted-U theory, performance is best at what level of arousal?
- Very low | Performance suffers if arousal is too low.
- Very high | Performance suffers if arousal is too high.
+ A moderate, optimal level
- It makes no difference | The theory says arousal level has a clear effect.

Q: What does cognitive anxiety refer to?
- A racing heart | A racing heart is a physical sign.
+ Worry and negative thoughts
- Sweaty palms | That is a physical sign.
- Tight muscles | That is a physical sign.

Q: Which is a physical (somatic) sign of anxiety?
- Doubting your ability | That is a thought, so it is cognitive.
- Worrying about losing | That is a thought, so it is cognitive.
+ A racing heart
- Thinking negative thoughts | That is cognitive anxiety.

Q: Which questionnaire is used to measure competitive state anxiety?
- PAR-Q | That is a safety questionnaire for starting exercise.
+ CSAI-2
- SMART | That is a goal setting method.
- FITT | That is a way to plan training.

Q: Which skill involves picturing yourself performing well?
- Self-talk | Self-talk uses words you say to yourself.
+ Imagery
- Relaxation | Relaxation uses breathing and muscle release.
- Goal setting | Goal setting is about planning targets.

Q: Which technique can help calm an athlete before a penalty?
- Hard sprinting | That would raise arousal even more.
+ Controlled breathing
- Eating a big meal | That does not help with nerves.
- Skipping the warm-up | That does not help with nerves.

Q: Which is an example of positive self-talk?
- I always mess this up | That is negative self-talk.
- Do not miss | That is a negative instruction focused on failing.
+ I am ready, stay calm and smooth
- This is too hard | That is negative self-talk.

Q: In SMART goals, what does the M stand for?
- Motivating | That is not the meaning of M here.
- Moderate | That is not the meaning of M here.
+ Measurable
- Manageable | That is not the meaning of M here.

Q: Which goal is the most SMART?
- Get fitter | This is not specific or measurable.
- Run faster someday | This has no number and no deadline.
+ Improve my 1500 m time from 8:00 to 7:30 within 8 weeks
- Train a lot | This is vague, with nothing to measure.
```
