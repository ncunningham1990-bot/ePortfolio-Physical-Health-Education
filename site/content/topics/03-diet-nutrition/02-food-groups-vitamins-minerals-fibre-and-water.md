- Explain the importance of **fibre** and **water**.

![Vitamins, minerals, fibre and water: jobs and food sources](images/micronutrients.svg)

## Vitamins and minerals (micronutrients)
We need these in **small amounts**, but they are essential for health.

| Nutrient | Why we need it | Food sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcium** | Strong bones and teeth, muscle contraction | Milk, cheese, yoghurt, fortified plant milks, leafy greens |
| **Iron** | Makes haemoglobin in red blood cells to carry oxygen | Red meat, beans, lentils, spinach, fortified cereals |
| **Vitamin C** | Healing, immune system, helps absorb iron | Citrus fruit, peppers, berries |
| **Vitamin D** | Helps the body absorb calcium for strong bones | Sunlight on skin, oily fish, eggs, fortified foods |
| **Vitamin A** | Vision and the immune system | Carrots, sweet potato, liver |
| **Sodium** | Fluid balance and nerve function (too much is unhealthy) | Salt, processed foods |

## Fibre
Fibre is a type of carbohydrate that the body cannot digest. It keeps the **digestive system healthy**, helps prevent constipation and helps you feel full. Sources: wholegrains, fruit, vegetables, beans and lentils.

## Water
Water makes up around **60%** of the body. It carries nutrients and waste, helps control body temperature through sweat, and keeps joints lubricated.

**Dehydration** (losing too much water) causes headaches, tiredness, poor concentration and reduced performance. Drink regularly during the day, and more in hot weather or when exercising. Pale yellow urine is a rough sign of good hydration.
