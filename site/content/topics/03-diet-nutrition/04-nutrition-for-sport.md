title: Nutrition for sport
summary: Eating and drinking before, during and after exercise.

## What you will learn
- Explain what to eat and drink before, during and after activity.
- Describe the role of hydration in performance.

## Before exercise
- Eat a meal rich in **carbohydrates** about 2-3 hours before, with some protein and not too much fat or fibre.
- If you eat closer to the activity, choose a small, easy-to-digest carbohydrate snack such as a banana.
- Start the activity **well hydrated**.
- On match days, avoid trying brand-new foods.

## During exercise
- For activity under about an hour, **water** is usually enough.
- For longer or very intense activity, a carbohydrate source (sports drink, fruit) can help maintain energy.
- Take small sips regularly. Don't wait until you are very thirsty.

## After exercise
- **Protein** helps muscles repair and adapt.
- **Carbohydrate** refills glycogen stores, especially if you train again soon.
- **Fluids** replace what was lost through sweat.
- A mix of carbohydrate and protein within a couple of hours is a good goal, for example a sandwich with eggs or chicken, or yoghurt with fruit.

## Endurance athletes
Some endurance athletes **carbohydrate load**, eating extra carbohydrate in the days before a long event to maximise glycogen. This isn't needed for most school sport.
