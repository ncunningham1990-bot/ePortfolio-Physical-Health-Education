## What you will learn
- Describe the functions of **carbohydrates, proteins and fats**.
- Give food sources for each.
- Compare how much energy each provides.

![Carbohydrates, proteins and fats with example foods and energy per gram](images/macronutrients.svg)

Carbohydrates, proteins and fats are **macronutrients**: we need them in **large amounts**.

| Nutrient | Main functions | Food sources | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates** | The body's main and quickest source of energy. Stored in muscles and liver as **glycogen** | Bread, rice, pasta, potatoes, oats, fruit | About **4 kcal** per gram |
| **Proteins** | **Growth and repair** of muscles and tissues. Also used to make enzymes and hormones | Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, lentils, tofu, nuts | About **4 kcal** per gram |
| **Fats** | **Energy store**, insulation, protecting organs, and absorbing vitamins A, D, E and K | Oils, nuts, seeds, avocado, oily fish, butter | About **9 kcal** per gram |

### Types of carbohydrate and fat
- **Complex carbohydrates** (wholegrains, oats, vegetables) release energy slowly. **Simple carbohydrates / sugars** (sweets, sugary drinks) release energy quickly.
- **Unsaturated fats** (olive oil, nuts, avocado, oily fish) are healthier choices. **Saturated fats** (fatty meat, butter, pastries) are best eaten in smaller amounts.
