## What you will learn
- Define a balanced diet.
- Describe how the plate model shows healthy proportions.
- Identify factors that influence food choices.

## What is a balanced diet?
A balanced diet contains **all the nutrients your body needs, in the right amounts**, from a **variety** of foods. No single food provides everything.

## The plate model
A simple way to think about a meal:
- About **half** the plate: vegetables and fruit.
- About **a quarter**: wholegrain carbohydrates (rice, pasta, bread, potatoes).
- About **a quarter**: protein foods (beans, fish, eggs, meat, tofu).
- A small amount of healthy fats, and water as the main drink.

Check the food guide from your own country or region for exact recommendations.

## What influences our food choices?
| Factor | Example |
|---|---|
| **Cost and access** | What is affordable and available nearby |
| **Culture and religion** | Traditional dishes, fasting, foods avoided |
| **Beliefs and values** | Vegetarian, vegan, ethical choices |
| **Health needs** | Allergies, intolerances, medical conditions |
| **Family and friends** | What others around you eat |
| **Media and advertising** | Social media, marketing, trends |
| **Skills and time** | Cooking skills, busy schedules |
