## What you will learn
- Name the three macronutrients, their functions and food sources.
- Compare how much energy each provides.

## The three macronutrients
Macronutrients are the nutrients we need in **large amounts**.

| Nutrient | Main functions | Food sources | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrates** | The body's main and fastest source of energy, stored in muscles and liver as glycogen | Bread, rice, pasta, potatoes, fruit, oats | About 4 kcal per gram |
| **Proteins** | Growth and repair of muscles and tissues; making enzymes and hormones | Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, lentils, tofu, nuts | About 4 kcal per gram |
| **Fats** | Energy store, insulation, protecting organs, absorbing vitamins A, D, E and K | Oils, nuts, seeds, avocado, oily fish, butter | About 9 kcal per gram |

### Types of fat
- **Unsaturated fats** (oils, nuts, avocado, oily fish) are healthier choices.
- **Saturated fats** (fatty meat, butter, pastries) are best eaten in smaller amounts.

### Carbohydrates in more detail
- **Complex carbohydrates** (wholegrains, oats, vegetables) release energy more slowly.
- **Simple carbohydrates / sugars** (sweets, sugary drinks) release energy quickly but do not last.
