## What you will learn
- Describe the role of key vitamins and minerals.
- Explain the importance of fibre and water.

## Micronutrients
Micronutrients are needed in **small amounts**, but they are essential.

| Nutrient | Why we need it | Food sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Calcium** | Strong bones and teeth, muscle contraction | Milk, cheese, yoghurt, fortified plant milks, leafy greens |
| **Iron** | Makes haemoglobin in red blood cells to carry oxygen | Red meat, beans, lentils, spinach, fortified cereals |
| **Vitamin C** | Healing, immune system, helps absorb iron | Citrus fruit, peppers, berries |
| **Vitamin D** | Helps the body absorb calcium | Sunlight on skin, oily fish, eggs, fortified foods |

## Fibre
Fibre is a type of carbohydrate that is not digested. It keeps the digestive system healthy and helps you feel full. Sources: wholegrains, fruit, vegetables, beans.

## Water
Water makes up around 60% of the body. It carries nutrients and waste, helps control body temperature through sweat, and keeps joints lubricated.

**Dehydration** (losing too much water) can cause headaches, tiredness, poor concentration and reduced performance, even at a mild level. Sip water regularly through the day, and drink more when exercising or in hot weather. Pale yellow urine is a rough sign of good hydration.
