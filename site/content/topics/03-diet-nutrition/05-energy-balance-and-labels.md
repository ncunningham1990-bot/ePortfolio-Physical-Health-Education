## Energy balance
Energy is measured in **kilocalories (kcal)** or **kilojoules (kJ)**.
- **Energy in:** the energy from the food and drink you consume.
- **Energy out:** the energy your body uses for basic functions (breathing, circulation, repair), plus movement and exercise.

When energy in equals energy out, weight stays stable. Growing teenagers and active people need plenty of energy. Skipping meals or following fad diets is not healthy for young people.

## Reading food labels
| Label feature | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Serving size** | Check whether numbers are per 100 g or per serving |
| **Energy (kcal/kJ)** | How much fuel a serving provides |
| **Sugars** | Choose foods lower in added sugar |
| **Saturated fat** | Lower is better |
| **Salt / sodium** | Lower is better |
| **Fibre** | Higher is better |
| **Ingredients list** | Listed from **largest to smallest** amount by weight |

Comparing the **per 100 g** column lets you compare two products fairly.

## A healthy relationship with food
Food is not only fuel; it is also part of culture and enjoyment. Eating regularly, listening to hunger and fullness, and eating a variety of foods supports both physical and mental health. If you are worried about your eating or your body image, talk to a parent, a teacher, a school nurse or a doctor.
