## What you will learn
- Define a **healthy balanced diet**.
- Use a plate model to plan healthy meals with examples from each food group.
- Describe factors that influence food choices.

## What is a balanced diet?
A **balanced diet** contains **all the nutrients the body needs, in the right amounts, from a variety of foods**. No single food gives everything.

## The plate model
![A balanced plate: fruit and vegetables, starchy carbohydrates, protein, dairy and oils](images/plate-model.svg)

- About **a third or more** of the plate: **fruit and vegetables**.
- About **a third**: **starchy carbohydrates**, preferably wholegrain (rice, pasta, bread, potatoes).
- Some **protein** foods (beans, fish, eggs, meat, tofu).
- Some **dairy or dairy alternatives** (milk, yoghurt, fortified plant milks).
- Small amounts of **unsaturated oils**; water as the main drink.
- Foods high in fat, salt or sugar: only occasionally.

Many countries publish their own food guide (for example the UK's **Eatwell Guide**). Your teacher can tell you which one your school uses. The proportions differ slightly but the ideas are the same.

## Example balanced meals
| Meal | Examples |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Porridge oats with milk and berries; wholegrain toast with eggs |
| Lunch | Chicken or bean wrap with salad; lentil soup with bread |
| Dinner | Grilled fish, rice and mixed vegetables; vegetable curry with brown rice |
| Snacks | Fruit, yoghurt, nuts, wholegrain crackers |

## What influences food choices?
| Factor | Example |
|---|---|
| **Cost and access** | What is affordable and available nearby |
| **Culture and religion** | Traditional dishes, fasting, foods avoided |
| **Beliefs and values** | Vegetarian, vegan or ethical choices |
| **Health needs** | Allergies, intolerances, medical conditions |
| **Family and friends** | What others around you eat |
| **Media and advertising** | Social media, marketing, trends |
| **Skills and time** | Cooking skills, busy schedules |
