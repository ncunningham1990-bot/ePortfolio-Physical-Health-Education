## What you will learn
- Define the ten joint movements.
- Give an example of each in sport.

![Ten movements at joints](images/joint-movements.svg)

| Movement | Definition | Example in sport |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexion** | Decreasing the angle at a joint | Bending the elbow in the upward phase of a bicep curl |
| **Extension** | Increasing the angle at a joint | Straightening the knee when kicking a ball |
| **Abduction** | Moving a limb **away** from the midline of the body | Raising the arm sideways in a star jump |
| **Adduction** | Moving a limb **towards** the midline | Bringing the legs together after a star jump |
| **Rotation** | Turning a bone around its long axis | Turning the head; the hip in a golf swing |
| **Circumduction** | A circular movement combining several movements | Arm circles in the butterfly or a cricket bowl |
| **Dorsiflexion** | Pulling the toes up towards the shin | The heel strike when running |
| **Plantar flexion** | Pointing the toes down | Pushing off to jump; pointing the toes in gymnastics |
| **Pronation** | Rotating the forearm so the palm faces **down** | Palm-down grip |
| **Supination** | Rotating the forearm so the palm faces **up** | Palm-up grip; catching soup in a bowl |

**Memory tip:** *Ab*duction takes the limb **ab**ay from the body; *ad*duction **ad**ds it back. *Supination:* "soup" bowl in your palm.

## Try it
First practise matching, then label the movements shown in the diagram.

```match
Bending the elbow = Flexion | Does the angle at the joint get smaller or bigger?
Straightening the knee = Extension | Does the angle at the joint get smaller or bigger?
Lifting the arm out to the side = Abduction | Is the arm moving away from or towards the body's midline?
Lowering the arm back to the side = Adduction | Is the arm moving away from or towards the body's midline?
Pointing the toes down = Plantar flexion | Think of the foot pressing like a gas pedal.
Pulling the toes up toward the shin = Dorsiflexion | The opposite of pointing the toes down.
Turning the palm down = Pronation | Think about the forearm rotating.
Turning the palm up = Supination | The opposite of palm down. Think of holding a bowl of soup.
```

```quiz
Q: A footballer swings the leg forward to kick. At the hip the thigh moves forward and the angle between the thigh and trunk decreases. What movement is this?
- Extension | Is the angle between the bones getting bigger or smaller?
+ Flexion
- Abduction | Abduction is sideways movement away from the midline.

Q: A swimmer lifts the arm out to the side at the shoulder. What is the movement?
- Adduction | Is the arm moving towards or away from the midline?
+ Abduction
- Circumduction | Circumduction is a circular movement. Is this a circle?

Q: A sprinter pushes off the ground by pointing the toes down. What is this movement?
- Dorsiflexion | Dorsiflexion pulls the toes up.
+ Plantar flexion
- Supination | Supination is a movement of the forearm.
```
