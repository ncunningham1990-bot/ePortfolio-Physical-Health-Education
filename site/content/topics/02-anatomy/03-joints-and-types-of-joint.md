## What you will learn
- Define a joint.
- Describe fixed, slightly moveable and freely moveable (synovial) joints with examples.

## Definition
A **joint** is a place where **two or more bones meet**.

![Fixed, slightly moveable and freely moveable joints](images/joint-classes.svg)

| Joint type | Movement | Structure | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** (fibrous) | None | Bones are joined by tough fibrous tissue | Plates of the cranium |
| **Slightly moveable** (cartilaginous) | Small | Bones are separated by a pad of cartilage | Between the vertebrae |
| **Freely moveable** (synovial) | Wide range | A fluid-filled capsule lets bones glide | Shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, wrist |

### Types of synovial joint
| Type | Movement | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Ball and socket** | All directions plus rotation | Shoulder, hip |
| **Hinge** | Bending and straightening only | Elbow, knee |
| **Pivot** | Rotation | Top of the neck (turning the head) |
| **Condyloid** | Movement in two planes without rotation | Wrist |
| **Gliding** | Small sliding movements | Between carpals and between vertebrae |
| **Saddle** | Back-and-forth and side-to-side | Base of the thumb |
