## What you will learn
- Name the main types of synovial joint and where they are found.
- Define the movements: flexion, extension, abduction, adduction, rotation and circumduction.

## Types of joint
Most movement happens at **synovial joints**, which have a fluid-filled capsule so the bones glide smoothly.

| Joint type | Movement allowed | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Ball and socket** | Movement in all directions, including rotation | Shoulder, hip |
| **Hinge** | Bending and straightening in one direction only | Elbow, knee |
| **Pivot** | Rotation | Top of the neck (turning the head) |
| **Gliding** | Small sliding movements | Between bones of the wrist and ankle |

## Movement terms
| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexion** | Decreasing the angle at a joint | Bending the elbow in a bicep curl |
| **Extension** | Increasing the angle at a joint | Straightening the knee when kicking |
| **Abduction** | Moving a limb away from the body's midline | Raising the arm out to the side |
| **Adduction** | Moving a limb towards the midline | Bringing the arm back to the side |
| **Rotation** | Turning around a long axis | Turning the head or twisting the hips |
| **Circumduction** | A circular movement | Arm circles when swimming butterfly |
