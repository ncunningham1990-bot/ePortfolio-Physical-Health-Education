## What you will learn
- Describe the **four functions** of the skeleton.
- Name the main bones and link them to a function.
- Distinguish the **axial** and **appendicular** skeleton.

## Four functions of the skeleton
| Function | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Shape and support** | Gives the body its shape and holds soft tissue up | The vertebral column holds the body upright |
| **Movement** | Bones act as levers; muscles pull on them across joints | The femur and tibia when you kick |
| **Protection** | Shields delicate organs | The cranium protects the brain; the ribs protect the heart and lungs; vertebrae protect the spinal cord |
| **Blood production** | Red and white blood cells are made in bone marrow | Marrow in the femur, pelvis and sternum |

(Some courses also list **storage of minerals** such as calcium.) An adult has **206 bones**.

## Axial and appendicular skeleton
- **Axial skeleton (green):** the central axis: **cranium, vertebral column, ribs and sternum**.
- **Appendicular skeleton (red):** the limbs and the girdles that attach them: **clavicle, scapula, humerus, radius, ulna, pelvis, femur, patella, tibia, fibula** and the hands and feet.

![Labelled skeleton showing the axial skeleton in green and the appendicular skeleton in red](images/skeleton-labelled.svg)

## Main bones to know
Cranium, vertebral column, sternum, ribs, clavicle, scapula, humerus, radius, ulna, carpals, metacarpals, phalanges, pelvis, femur, patella, tibia, fibula, tarsals, metatarsals.

## Try it
Use the diagram above to help you at first. Then try the labelling task from memory.

```label
image: images/skeleton-numbered.svg
alt: Skeleton with twelve numbered bones to label
1 = Cranium | This bone protects the brain.
2 = Clavicle | This is the bone at the top of the chest between the sternum and shoulder, often called the collarbone.
3 = Humerus | This is the bone of the upper arm.
4 = Radius and ulna | Two bones sit side by side in the forearm.
5 = Pelvis | This bone group forms a bowl shape joining the spine to the legs.
6 = Femur | This is the longest bone in the body, in the thigh.
7 = Tibia | This is the larger weight-bearing bone of the lower leg.
8 = Vertebral column | A stack of small bones that protects the spinal cord.
9 = Sternum | A flat bone in the middle of the chest that the ribs attach to.
10 = Ribs | Curved bones that protect the heart and lungs.
11 = Patella | A small bone at the front of the knee.
12 = Fibula | The thinner bone of the lower leg, beside the tibia.
```
