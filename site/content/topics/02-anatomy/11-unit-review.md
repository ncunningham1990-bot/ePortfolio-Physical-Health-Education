Use this to check what you remember from the whole unit. If something is wrong, read the hint, think about it, and try again.

```quiz
Q: Which bones belong to the axial skeleton?
- Humerus, femur, tibia | Which part of the skeleton are the limb bones in?
+ Cranium, vertebrae, ribs, sternum
- Clavicle, pelvis, patella | Check whether these belong to the limbs.

Q: Which type of bone is the sternum?
- Long | Is the sternum longer than it is wide?
+ Flat
- Short | Is the sternum cube-shaped?

Q: Which type of joint is the knee?
- Ball and socket | Does the knee move in every direction?
+ Hinge
- Pivot | Does the knee mainly rotate?

Q: Which part of a synovial joint produces synovial fluid?
- Articular cartilage | Cartilage covers bone ends.
+ Synovial membrane
- Ligament | Ligaments join bone to bone.

Q: The muscle attachment on the bone that moves is the...
- origin | The origin is on the bone that stays still.
+ insertion

Q: Which muscle fibre type is best for a marathon?
+ Slow twitch
- Fast twitch | Fast twitch fibres tire quickly.

Q: In the downward phase of a bicep curl, the bicep lengthens under load. Which contraction?
- Concentric | Is the muscle shortening?
- Isometric | Does the muscle length stay the same?
+ Eccentric
```
```match
Flexion = Decreasing the angle at a joint | Does the angle get smaller or bigger?
Extension = Increasing the angle at a joint | Does the angle get smaller or bigger?
Abduction = Moving away from the midline | Think about which prefix means 'away'.
Adduction = Moving towards the midline | Think about which prefix means 'add to'.
Agonist = Muscle that contracts to cause movement | Which muscle does the work?
Antagonist = Muscle that relaxes during movement | Which muscle gives way?
Tendon = Joins muscle to bone | Which tissue connects muscle to bone?
Ligament = Joins bone to bone | Which tissue connects bone to bone?
```
