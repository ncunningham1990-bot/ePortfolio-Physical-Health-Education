## What you will learn
- Trace the path of air into the lungs.
- Explain how gas exchange happens in the alveoli.
- Describe how breathing changes during exercise.

## Path of air
Nose or mouth → **trachea** (windpipe) → **bronchi** → **bronchioles** → **alveoli** (tiny air sacs).

## Gas exchange
The alveoli are surrounded by capillaries. **Oxygen** moves from the air in the alveoli into the blood, and **carbon dioxide** moves from the blood into the alveoli to be breathed out. Gases move by **diffusion**, from where there is more of them to where there is less. Alveoli have thin walls and a huge surface area, which makes exchange fast.

## How we breathe
| | Diaphragm | Ribs | Chest volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Breathing in** | Contracts and flattens | Move up and out | Increases, air rushes in |
| **Breathing out** | Relaxes and moves up | Move down and in | Decreases, air pushed out |

## Exercise and breathing
During exercise your muscles use more oxygen and make more carbon dioxide, so your **breathing rate** and **depth** increase. Regular training strengthens breathing muscles and improves how efficiently the lungs work.
