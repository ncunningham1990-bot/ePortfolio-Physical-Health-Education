## What you will learn
- Describe the structure of the heart and the path of blood.
- Compare arteries, veins and capillaries.
- Explain short-term and long-term effects of exercise.

## The heart
The heart is a muscular pump with **four chambers**: two **atria** (top, receive blood) and two **ventricles** (bottom, pump blood out).
- The **right side** pumps oxygen-poor blood to the **lungs**.
- The **left side** pumps oxygen-rich blood to the **body**. It has thicker walls because it pushes blood further.

## Blood vessels
| Vessel | Carries blood | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Arteries** | **Away** from the heart | Thick, elastic walls to cope with high pressure |
| **Veins** | **Towards** the heart | Thinner walls and valves to stop backflow |
| **Capillaries** | Between arteries and veins | One cell thick; oxygen and nutrients pass into tissues here |

## Blood
**Red blood cells** carry oxygen, **white blood cells** fight infection, **platelets** help blood clot, and **plasma** is the liquid that carries everything.

## Effects of exercise
- **Short term:** heart rate increases and the heart pumps more blood per beat (**stroke volume**). Cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume.
- **Long term:** the heart muscle becomes stronger, **resting heart rate falls**, and blood vessels improve, so oxygen is delivered more efficiently.
