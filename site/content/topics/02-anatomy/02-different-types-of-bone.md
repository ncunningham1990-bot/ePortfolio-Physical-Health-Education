## What you will learn
- Name the four types of bone.
- Give an example of each and link shape to function.

![Long, short, flat and irregular bones with examples](images/bone-types.svg)

| Type | Shape | Examples | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long** | Longer than wide, with a shaft and two ends | Femur, humerus, tibia, radius, ulna, phalanges | Act as **levers** for movement and make blood cells in the marrow |
| **Short** | Roughly cube-shaped | Carpals (wrist), tarsals (ankle) | Give **stability** with some small movement |
| **Flat** | Thin and often curved | Cranium, sternum, ribs, scapula, pelvis | **Protect** organs and give a large surface for **muscle attachment** |
| **Irregular** | Complex shapes that do not fit other groups | Vertebrae | **Protection** (spinal cord) and support |

Shape follows function: a long femur is a good lever, a curved cranium is a strong shield.

## Try it
```match
Femur = Long bone | Is this bone much longer than it is wide?
Carpals = Short bone | These small bones in the wrist are roughly cube-shaped.
Sternum = Flat bone | Is this bone thin and broad rather than long?
Vertebra = Irregular bone | Which bone type has a shape that does not fit the other groups?

```
