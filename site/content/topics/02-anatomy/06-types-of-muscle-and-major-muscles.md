## What you will learn
- Describe smooth, cardiac and skeletal muscle.
- Name and locate the 11 major muscles and describe the movement each produces.

## Three types of muscle
![Skeletal, cardiac and smooth muscle](images/muscle-types.svg)

| Type | Control | Where | Appearance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skeletal** | **Voluntary** (you choose) | Attached to bones by tendons: biceps, quadriceps | Long, striped (striated) fibres |
| **Cardiac** | **Involuntary** | Only the heart wall | Striped, branched cells joined by intercalated discs. Never tires |
| **Smooth** | **Involuntary** | Walls of the gut, blood vessels, airways | Spindle-shaped cells, no stripes. Works slowly and does not tire easily |

Skeletal muscle is the type that moves the body in sport, so the rest of this unit focuses on it.

## The 11 major skeletal muscles
| Muscle | Main job | Sporting example |
|---|---|---|
| Bicep | Bends the arm at the elbow (elbow flexion) | The upward phase of a bicep curl |
| Tricep | Straightens the arm at the elbow (elbow extension) | Basketball chest pass |
| Quadriceps | Straightens the leg at the knee (knee extension) | Striking a football |
| Hamstrings | Bends the leg at the knee (knee flexion) | Bringing the leg back before a kick |
| Latissimus dorsi | Pulls the arm down and back (shoulder extension and adduction) | The pull phase of front crawl |
| Deltoid | Lifts the arm away from the body (shoulder abduction) | Raising the arms to block in volleyball |
| Abdominals | Bends the trunk forward (trunk flexion) | Tuck in a forward roll |
| Gastrocnemius | Points the toes and lifts the heel (plantar flexion) | Pushing off to jump for a rebound |
| Gluteus maximus | Pulls the leg back at the hip (hip extension) | Driving up from a squat |
| Pectorals | Pulls the arm across the front of the body (shoulder horizontal adduction) | A tennis forehand |
| Trapezius | Lifts and pulls back the shoulder blades (scapula elevation and retraction) | Pulling the shoulders back at the finish of a rowing stroke |

## Try it
Use the **interactive activity** (button above) to practise locating each muscle, then try these tasks.

```match
Skeletal muscle = Voluntary and attached to bones | Which muscle type do you control?
Cardiac muscle = Found only in the heart | Which muscle type never stops working?
Smooth muscle = Found in the gut and blood vessel walls | Which muscle type is involuntary and has spindle-shaped cells?
```
```match
Straightens the leg at the knee = Quadriceps | Think about the muscle on the front of the thigh.
Bends the leg at the knee = Hamstrings | Think about the muscle on the back of the thigh.
Bends the arm at the elbow = Bicep | Think about the front of the upper arm.
Straightens the arm at the elbow = Tricep | Think about the back of the upper arm.
Lifts the arm away from the body = Deltoid | Think about the muscle that caps the shoulder.
Pulls the leg back at the hip = Gluteus maximus | Think about the largest muscle in the buttocks.
Points the toes and lifts the heel = Gastrocnemius | Think about the calf muscle.
Bends the trunk forward = Abdominals | Think about the muscles at the front of the trunk.
```
