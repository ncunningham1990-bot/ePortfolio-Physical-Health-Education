## What you will learn
- Define slow twitch and fast twitch muscle fibres.
- Compare their characteristics and link them to activities.

![Comparison of slow twitch and fast twitch fibres](images/fibre-types.svg)

| | **Slow twitch (Type I)** | **Fast twitch (Type II)** |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Red (lots of myoglobin and blood supply) | Paler / white |
| Contraction speed | Slow | Fast |
| Force produced | Low | High |
| Resistance to fatigue | High (tire slowly) | Low (tire quickly) |
| Energy system | Mainly aerobic (uses oxygen) | Mainly anaerobic (without oxygen) |
| Best for | Endurance and posture: marathon running, long-distance cycling | Speed and power: sprinting, jumping, throwing, weightlifting |

Most people have a **mix** of fibre types. The proportion is partly inherited, and training can change how the fibres perform.

