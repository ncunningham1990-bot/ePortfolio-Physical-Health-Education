## What you will learn
- Name the 11 major muscles and locate them on the body.
- Describe the movement each muscle produces.
- Link each muscle to a sporting movement.

## Key ideas
Muscles work by **contracting** (getting shorter) to pull on bones. Muscles can only pull, never push, so they work in pairs: when one contracts to bend a joint, its partner relaxes.

| Muscle | Main job | Sporting example |
|---|---|---|
| Bicep | Bends the arm at the elbow | Upward phase of a bicep curl |
| Tricep | Straightens the arm at the elbow | Basketball chest pass |
| Quadriceps | Straightens the leg at the knee | Striking a football |
| Hamstrings | Bends the leg at the knee | Bringing the leg back before a kick |
| Latissimus Dorsi | Pulls the arm down and back | Front-crawl pull phase |
| Deltoid | Lifts the arm away from the body | Blocking in volleyball |
| Abdominals | Bends the trunk forward | Tuck in a forward roll |
| Gastrocnemius | Points the toes, lifts the heel | Jumping for a rebound |
| Gluteus Maximus | Pulls the leg back at the hip | Driving up from a squat |
| Pectorals | Pulls the arm across the body | Tennis forehand |
| Trapezius | Lifts and pulls back the shoulder blades | Rowing finish |
