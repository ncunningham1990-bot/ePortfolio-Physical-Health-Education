## Definition of fitness
**Fitness** is the ability to meet the demands of your environment and daily life (and your sport) without becoming overly tired, and with energy left over to enjoy other activities. Fitness is not one thing. It is made up of several **components**, and different sports need different mixes.

![The 11 components of fitness: five health-related and six skill-related](images/components-of-fitness.svg)

## The 11 components
| Component | Definition | Sporting example |
|---|---|---|
| **Cardiovascular endurance** | The ability of the heart and lungs to supply oxygen to working muscles for a long period | Distance running, cycling |
| **Body composition** | The proportion of fat, muscle, bone and water in the body | Lean mass for a gymnast |
| **Flexibility** | The range of movement possible at a joint | A gymnast's splits |
| **Muscular endurance** | The ability of a muscle (or group) to keep contracting repeatedly without tiring | Rowing, many push-ups |
| **Speed** | How quickly you can move your body (or a body part) over a distance | A 100 m sprint |
| **Strength** | The maximum force a muscle can produce in one effort | A one-rep-max lift |
| **Agility** | Changing direction quickly while keeping control | Dodging a defender |
| **Balance** | Keeping the centre of mass over the base of support, still or moving | A gymnast on a beam |
| **Co-ordination** | Using senses and body parts together smoothly and accurately | Hitting a ball with a bat |
| **Reaction time** | The time between a signal and the start of your response | Reacting to the starter's gun |
| **Power** | Force produced quickly (strength × speed) | A standing jump, a sprint start |

Components 1–5 are often grouped as **health-related** (important for everyone's health). Components 6–11 are **skill-related** (they help you perform in sport). Notice that **speed** and **strength** can appear in either group depending on the course, so always check your own handbook.

Agility :: Changing direction quickly while staying in control

```match
Marathon running = Cardiovascular endurance | Think about which activity lasts the longest and relies on oxygen being delivered for a long time.
A gymnast doing the splits = Flexibility | Which component is about how far a joint can move?
A footballer dodging a tackler = Agility | This involves quickly changing direction. Which component names that?
A standing long jump = Power | The jump needs force AND speed together. Which component combines the two?
Catching a ruler dropped without warning = Reaction time | What is being measured: how hard, how far or how fast you respond to a signal?
Holding a handstand on a beam = Balance | Which component is about staying stable over a base of support?
```
