## What you will learn
- Describe the three parts of a warm-up and why they matter.
- Describe a cool-down and its benefits.

## Warm-up: prepare the body
A good warm-up has three stages:

1. **Pulse raiser:** light activity such as jogging that gradually raises heart rate and body temperature.
2. **Mobility and dynamic stretching:** moving through a full range of motion (leg swings, arm circles, lunges with a twist).
3. **Sport-specific practice:** drills that copy the movements of the activity (passing, short sprints, practice shots).

**Benefits:** warmer muscles work better, increased blood flow brings oxygen to muscles, joints move more freely, the risk of injury is reduced, and you get mentally ready.

## Cool-down: recover safely
1. **Gradually reduce activity:** a slow jog or walk so heart rate and breathing come down steadily.
2. **Static stretching:** hold each major muscle stretch for about 15-30 seconds while the muscles are warm.

**Benefits:** helps the body return gradually to its resting state, can reduce muscle stiffness, and is a good time to improve flexibility.
