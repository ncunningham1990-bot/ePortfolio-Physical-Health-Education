## What you will learn
- Define VO₂ max.
- Explain why it is a measure of cardiovascular endurance.
- Describe how it can be measured or estimated.

## What is VO₂ max?
**VO₂ max** is the **maximum volume of oxygen** (in millilitres) that your working muscles can take in, transport and use **per kilogram of body mass per minute**. It is written in **ml/kg/min**.

A higher VO₂ max means your heart, lungs and blood can deliver oxygen more efficiently, so you can keep going at a higher intensity. That is why it is the standard measure of **cardiovascular endurance**.

![Illustrative VO₂ max ranges for untrained teenagers up to elite endurance athletes](images/chart-vo2max.svg)

## How is it measured?
- **Laboratory test (gold standard):** you run or cycle with increasing difficulty to exhaustion while wearing a mask that measures the oxygen you breathe in and the carbon dioxide you breathe out.
- **Field tests (estimates):** the **multistage fitness (bleep) test** and the **Cooper 12-minute run** estimate VO₂ max from your score. They are cheaper and easier but less exact.

## What affects VO₂ max?
Genetics, age, sex, training (it can improve with regular aerobic training), body composition, altitude and health.
