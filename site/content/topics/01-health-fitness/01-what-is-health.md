## What you will learn
- Define health and wellbeing.
- Describe the physical, mental and social aspects of health.
- Explain how exercise can affect each aspect.

## Key ideas
The World Health Organization describes health as *a state of complete physical, mental and social wellbeing, and not merely the absence of disease or illness*. Being healthy is about more than not being sick.

| Aspect | What it means | How exercise can help |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | How well your body works: heart, lungs, muscles, bones | Stronger heart and muscles, healthier weight, better sleep |
| **Mental (emotional)** | How you think, feel and cope with stress | Reduces stress and anxiety, boosts mood and confidence |
| **Social** | Your relationships and sense of belonging | Teamwork, friendships, feeling part of a group |

These aspects are linked. A problem in one (for example, being stressed) can affect the others (poor sleep, withdrawing from friends).

### Things that affect health
Sleep, diet, physical activity, stress, relationships, family and community, access to healthcare, and the environment you live in.
