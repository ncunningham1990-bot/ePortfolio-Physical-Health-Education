## What you will learn
- Describe a range of **physical, psychological and social** factors that affect fitness and performance.
- Explain how a factor can help or limit performance.

![Physical, psychological and social factors that affect fitness](images/factors-affecting-fitness.svg)

## Physical factors
- **Age and sex:** fitness changes through life. Hormones affect muscle and body fat.
- **Genetics:** body type, muscle fibre types and VO₂ max potential are partly inherited.
- **Diet and hydration:** the right fuel and fluids support training.
- **Sleep and recovery:** the body adapts and repairs while resting.
- **Health, illness and injury:** these can stop or reduce training.
- **Lifestyle:** smoking, alcohol and inactivity reduce fitness.
- **Training and environment:** the type of training, heat, cold and altitude.

## Psychological factors
- **Motivation** to start and keep going.
- **Stress and anxiety** can affect sleep, energy and focus.
- **Confidence and attitude** to exercise and to challenges.
- **Mood and mental health.**

## Social factors
- **Family and friends:** support or pressure to be active.
- **Peers and role models.**
- **Culture and beliefs** about sport and exercise.
- **Access to facilities, equipment, coaching and clubs** (and the cost).
- **Time:** school, work and family commitments.
- **Media and social media** influences.
