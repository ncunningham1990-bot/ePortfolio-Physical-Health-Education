Use this to check what you remember. Aim for full marks, and revisit any lesson where you slip up.

```quiz
Q: Which is NOT one of the three aspects of health?
- Physical
- Mental
- Social
+ Financial
? The three aspects are physical, mental and social.

Q: Which component of fitness is the range of movement at a joint?
+ Flexibility
- Power
- Balance

Q: A rugby player's muscles work hard in short bursts to produce force quickly. Which component is this?
- Flexibility
+ Power
- Body composition

Q: Increasing your training from 3 to 4 days a week changes which part of FITT?
+ Frequency
- Intensity
- Type

Q: Which test measures cardiovascular endurance?
- Sit-and-reach
- Standing long jump
+ Multistage fitness (bleep) test

Q: Which is the correct order for a warm-up?
- Sport-specific drills, stretching, pulse raiser
+ Pulse raiser, mobility/dynamic stretching, sport-specific drills
- Static stretching, rest, sprint
```
```match
Specificity = Training matches the sport
Reversibility = Fitness is lost when you stop
Progressive overload = Gradually increasing the demand
Agility = Changing direction quickly
Body composition = Proportion of fat, muscle, bone and water
```
```fill
The five health-related components include flexibility, muscular {strength} and body {composition}.
The ruler drop test measures {reaction} time.
```
