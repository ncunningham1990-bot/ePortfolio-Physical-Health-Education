## What you will learn
- Name the health-related and skill-related components of fitness.
- Define each component and link it to a sporting example.

## Health-related components
These matter for everyone's general health.

| Component | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Cardiovascular endurance** | Ability of the heart and lungs to supply oxygen to the muscles for a long time | Distance running |
| **Muscular strength** | The most force a muscle can produce in one effort | A one-rep maximum lift |
| **Muscular endurance** | Ability of a muscle to keep working over time without tiring | Many push-ups in a row |
| **Flexibility** | The range of movement around a joint | Doing the splits in gymnastics |
| **Body composition** | The proportion of fat, muscle, bone and water in the body | Lean muscle mass versus body fat |

## Skill-related components
These help performance in sport.

| Component | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Agility** | Changing direction quickly while keeping control | Dodging a defender |
| **Balance** | Keeping the body stable, still or moving | A gymnast on a beam |
| **Coordination** | Using senses and body parts together smoothly | Hitting a ball with a bat |
| **Power** | Strength × speed: force produced quickly | A standing jump, a sprint start |
| **Reaction time** | How fast you respond to a stimulus | Reacting to the starter's gun |
| **Speed** | How fast you can move over a distance | A 100 m sprint |

Different sports need different mixes of components. A marathon runner relies on cardiovascular endurance; a shot putter relies on power and strength.
