## What you will learn
- Describe the four essential elements of a general training session.
- Explain the purpose of each element.

![A training session: warm-up, endurance training, resistance training, cool-down](images/training-session-elements.svg)

## 1. Warm-up and stretching
1. **Pulse raiser:** light activity such as jogging to raise heart rate and body temperature.
2. **Mobility and dynamic stretching:** leg swings, arm circles, walking lunges.
3. **Sport-specific practice:** drills that copy the movements to come.

**Why:** warmer muscles work better, joints move freely, blood flow increases and the risk of injury is lower. You also get mentally ready.

## 2. Endurance training
Training the **cardiovascular system** with continuous, interval or fartlek work (for example jogging, swimming, cycling). It improves how well the heart, lungs and blood deliver oxygen.

## 3. Resistance training
Training that makes muscles work against a **resistance** (body weight, free weights, machines, bands). It builds **strength** and **muscular endurance** and helps protect joints.

## 4. Cool-down
1. **Gradually reduce activity** (slow jog or walk) so heart rate and breathing come down.
2. **Static stretching:** hold each major muscle stretch for about 15-30 seconds.

**Why:** helps the body return to its resting state, reduces stiffness and is a good time to improve flexibility.
