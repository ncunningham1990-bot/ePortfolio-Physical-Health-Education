## What you will learn
- Describe four psychological skills: **mental imagery, breathing techniques, muscle relaxation and self-talk**.
- Explain how each can control arousal and improve performance.

![The PST toolkit: imagery, breathing, relaxation, self-talk](images/pst-toolkit.svg)

**Psychological skills training (PST)** is regular practice of mental techniques, just as you practise physical skills.

## 1. Mental imagery
Picturing yourself performing a skill successfully, using **all senses** (what you see, hear and feel).
- **Internal imagery:** from your own point of view.
- **External imagery:** watching yourself like a video.
- Use it to rehearse skills, build confidence and prepare for a competition. Practise it regularly and keep it realistic and positive.

## 2. Breathing techniques
Slow, controlled breathing lowers heart rate and calms the body. In **box breathing** you breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out for 4 and hold for 4.

![Box breathing: in for 4, hold 4, out for 4, hold 4](images/box-breathing.svg)

## 3. Muscle relaxation
**Progressive muscle relaxation** means **tensing** a muscle group for a few seconds, then **releasing** it, and working through the body (feet, legs, stomach, arms, shoulders, face). It reduces **somatic** (physical) anxiety and helps sleep.

## 4. Self-talk
What you say to yourself, silently or aloud.
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Positive** | "I've trained for this." |
| **Instructional** | "Eyes on the ball." "Elbow up." |
| **Motivational** | "Keep pushing!" |
| **Negative (avoid)** | "I always miss." |

Change negative thoughts into helpful ones: "Don't mess up" becomes "Focus on a smooth release." Short **cue words** ("smooth", "explode") help you refocus.
