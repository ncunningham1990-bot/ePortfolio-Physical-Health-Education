## Definition
**Motivation** is the **drive** that makes us start an activity, keep going when it is hard, and try our best.

![Intrinsic and extrinsic motivation](images/motivation-types.svg)

| Type | Where it comes from | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic** | From **within**: enjoyment, challenge, personal satisfaction | Playing because it is fun; wanting to master a skill |
| **Extrinsic** | From **outside**: rewards or pressure | Trophies, medals, prizes, praise, avoiding punishment |

Extrinsic rewards can be **tangible** (trophies, money) or **intangible** (praise, recognition). Both types can be useful. Intrinsic motivation tends to last longer. If rewards become the only reason to take part, enjoyment can fall when the rewards stop.

### Building motivation
- Set clear, achievable goals and track progress.
- Choose activities you enjoy and add variety.
- Train with friends or a team.
- Celebrate effort and improvement, not only winning.
- Remind yourself *why* you started.
