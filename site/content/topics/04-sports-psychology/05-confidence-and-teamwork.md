## What you will learn
- Define self-confidence and self-efficacy.
- Describe ways to build confidence.
- Explain task and social cohesion in teams.

## Self-confidence
Self-confidence is your belief that you can perform well. **Self-efficacy** is confidence in a specific task ("I can hit this serve"). Four ways to build it:

| Source | Example |
|---|---|
| **Past success** | Remembering previous good performances |
| **Watching others succeed (modelling)** | Seeing a teammate complete the skill |
| **Encouragement (verbal persuasion)** | "You've got this," from a coach or team |
| **Feeling calm and ready (managing emotions)** | Breathing and positive routines |

Over-confidence can be a problem too: it may lead to poor preparation.

## Team cohesion
Cohesion is how well a team **sticks together** to pursue its goals.
- **Task cohesion:** working together towards shared goals and roles.
- **Social cohesion:** liking and supporting each other.

Cohesive teams communicate well, share goals, accept roles, and support each other after mistakes. Both types help, but the balance varies between teams and sports.

## Building team cohesion
- Agree on team goals together.
- Make sure every player has a clear role and feels valued.
- Practise communication and positive encouragement.
- Team-building activities and social events.
- Handle conflict openly and fairly.
