## What you will learn
- Describe the three **somatotypes** (body types).
- Link body types to sports, and explain the limits of the model.

![Ectomorph, mesomorph and endomorph body types](images/somatotypes.svg)

| Somatotype | Features | Suits (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ectomorph** | Lean, narrow shoulders and hips, long limbs, low body fat and muscle | Long-distance running, high jump, basketball |
| **Mesomorph** | Muscular, wide shoulders, narrow waist, athletic build, gains muscle easily | Sprinting, gymnastics, weightlifting, swimming |
| **Endomorph** | Rounder shape, wider hips, higher body fat, stores fat easily | Shot put, sumo wrestling, prop forward |

### Important points
- Most people are a **mixture** of the three types (rated on a scale for each) rather than a perfect single type.
- Body type is only **one factor** in performance. Skill, training, motivation, diet and psychology matter a lot.
- Any body can enjoy sport and be healthy. Body shape does **not** decide your worth, and the "best" body type for a sport depends on its demands.
- Concerns about body image are common. If you are worried, talk to a trusted adult.
