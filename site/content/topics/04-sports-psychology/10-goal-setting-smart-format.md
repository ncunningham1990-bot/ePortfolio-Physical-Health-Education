## What you will learn
- Write goals in **SMART** format.
- Explain why goals improve motivation and performance.
- Distinguish outcome, performance and process goals.

![SMART goals](images/smart-goals.svg)

| Letter | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Specific**: clear and detailed | Improve my 1500 m time |
| **M** | **Measurable**: you can track it with numbers | From 8:00 to 7:30 |
| **A** | **Achievable**: challenging but realistic | A 30-second improvement is realistic |
| **R** | **Relevant**: matters to you and your sport | Helps me make the school team |
| **T** | **Time-bound**: has a deadline | Within 8 weeks |

**Example SMART goal:** *"Improve my 1500 m time from 8:00 to 7:30 within 8 weeks by training three times a week."*

### Why set goals?
Goals give **direction**, increase **motivation and focus**, let you **measure progress**, and build **confidence** when you achieve them.

### Types of goal
| Type | Focus | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Outcome** | The final result (depends on others) | Win the tournament |
| **Performance** | Your own standard | Run 1500 m in under 7:30 |
| **Process** | The actions needed to perform well | Keep my head up and arms relaxed when running |

Break a **long-term goal** into **short-term goals** (stepping stones), review progress often and adjust goals when needed.
