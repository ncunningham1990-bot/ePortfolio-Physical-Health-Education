## What you will learn
- Define **health** and **well-being**.
- Describe **physical, mental and social** well-being.
- Explain how exercise can improve each aspect.

## Definitions
The World Health Organization describes **health** as *a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity*.

**Well-being** is how well you are doing and feeling overall. It has three linked parts:

![Health and well-being: physical, mental and social](images/wellbeing-triangle.svg)

| Aspect | What it means | How exercise can help |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | How well your body works: heart, lungs, muscles, bones, energy, sleep | Stronger heart and muscles, healthy body composition, better sleep |
| **Mental (emotional)** | How you think and feel and how you cope with stress | Reduces stress and anxiety, boosts mood and confidence |
| **Social** | Your relationships and sense of belonging | Teamwork, friendships, feeling part of a group |

The three parts affect each other. For example, stress (mental) can cause poor sleep (physical) and make you avoid friends (social). Looking after one part helps the others.
