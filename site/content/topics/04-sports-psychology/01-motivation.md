## What is motivation?
Motivation is the **drive** that makes us start an activity, keep going when it's hard, and try our best.

| Type | Where it comes from | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic** | From within: enjoyment, challenge, personal satisfaction | Playing because it's fun; wanting to master a skill |
| **Extrinsic** | From outside: rewards or pressure | Trophies, medals, praise, prize money, avoiding punishment |

Both can be useful. Intrinsic motivation tends to last longer. If rewards become the only reason to take part, enjoyment can fall when the rewards stop.

## Building motivation
- Set clear, achievable goals and track progress.
- Choose activities you enjoy; add variety.
- Train with friends or a team for support.
- Celebrate effort and improvement, not only winning.
- Remind yourself *why* you started.
