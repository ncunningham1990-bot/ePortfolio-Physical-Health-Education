## Rubric: Criterion A, Knowing and Understanding

Find the level that best matches your work, then look at the level above to see how to improve.

| Level | Task-specific clarification |
|---|---|
| **1-2** | From your client's initial physical performance, you submit a **list** that includes:<br>• current ability in the desired skill, and/or<br>• strengths and weaknesses, and/or<br>• features of correct skill execution.<br><br>From your client's initial health assessment, you state through a **list**:<br>• aspects of well-being, and/or<br>• strengths and weaknesses, and/or<br>• relationship to physical performance.<br><br>You suggest inappropriate, brief and/or basic solutions to the client's selected areas for improvement. You rarely use subject-specific terminology. |
| **3-4** | From your client's initial physical performance, you submit a **brief account** of:<br>• current ability in the desired skill, and/or<br>• strengths and weaknesses, and/or<br>• features of correct skill execution.<br><br>From your client's initial health assessment, you submit a **brief account** of:<br>• basic aspects of well-being, and/or<br>• basic strengths and weaknesses, and/or<br>• basic relationship to physical performance.<br><br>You partially investigate the issues highlighted by your client and present solutions that may be superficial, inappropriate or incomplete. You use some appropriate subject-specific terminology. |
| **5-6** | From your client's initial physical performance, you submit a brief account of **distinguishing features** of:<br>• current ability in the desired skill<br>• strengths and weaknesses<br>• features of correct skill execution.<br><br>From your client's initial health assessment, you submit a brief account of **distinguishing features** of:<br>• aspects of well-being<br>• strengths and weaknesses<br>• relationship to physical performance.<br><br>You use your knowledge (from strand A(i)) to investigate the issues highlighted by the client and present solutions that are mostly relevant. You frequently use appropriate subject-specific terminology. |
| **7-8** | From your client's initial physical performance, you submit a **detailed account that justifies** your determination of:<br>• current ability in the desired skill<br>• strengths and weaknesses<br>• features of correct skill execution.<br><br>From your client's initial health assessment, you submit a **detailed account that justifies** your determination of:<br>• current well-being<br>• strengths and weaknesses<br>• relationship to physical performance.<br><br>You use knowledge from strand A(i) to **investigate in depth** the issues highlighted by the client and develop relevant solutions. You consistently use most or all of the relevant subject-specific terminology. |

Not sure what command words like *justify* or *analyse* mean? See the [command terms on the rubric page](#tasks/0).
