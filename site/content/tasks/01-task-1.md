### HEALTH ANALYSIS

*Explain in detail your client's current level of health and wellbeing. Use the interview and questionnaires to establish this information.*

#### Client profile, interview and PAR-Q

*Introduce your client and explain what the interview and PAR-Q told you. Do not paste the full interview.*

- *Summary of interview answers about health, well-being and fitness*
- *Summary of PAR-Q responses and what it showed* · [Online PAR-Q form](https://coachway.io/templates/par-q-form/)
- *Age, height, weight and BMI*
- *Previous injury, illness or recent surgery*
- *Diet, calorie and water intake*
- *Sleep and recovery days*
- *Activity level and steps per day*

#### Measurements of health and well-being

*Say what you used (for example surveys, scales, interviews or observation) and why. Fill in the table below, adding rows if you need them, then analyse what the results tell you. You can add a graph or screenshot too.*

- *Each test named, with why you chose it*
- *The scoring scale or mark scheme*
- *What the results mean for your client*
- *Referenced sources*

**Table columns in your document:** Test and purpose, Scoring scale, My client's results, Analysis.

*Use what you have learned in lessons and your own research to explain your findings.*

- *Theory named correctly (for example the Inverted U, motivation, anxiety, arousal, confidence, stress)*
- *Nutrition, sleep and recovery explained*
- *Theory linked to your client's results*
- *Referenced sources*

*Comment on your client's overall health and fitness. Then evaluate how these things could affect their performance in the chosen event.*

- *Strengths and weaknesses in health and well-being*
- *How health relates to physical performance*
- *Research and references to support your points*

### PHYSICAL ANALYSIS

*Explain in detail your client's current level of physical abilities, including components of fitness and their current level of ability and technique in their chosen Athletics event.*

#### Chosen sport

*Introduce and explain your client's chosen athletics event. Reference your sources.*

- *What the event involves (technique, phases and strategies)*
- *World records (factual information)*
- *References*

#### Fitness analysis

*Show your client's results for each test in the table, adding rows if you need them.*

- *3 trials for each test and the average of each fitness test*
- *3 trials for each test and the average of their athletic event initial performance*
- *What the results show about your client's current level and what needs improved*

**Table columns in your document:** Fitness test and purpose, Trial 1, Trial 2, Trial 3, Average, Analysis.

#### Technique analysis

*Record and analyse your client's initial performance technique in their athletics event. In the table below, insert screenshots of your client and a 'perfect model' (for example an Olympic athlete or world champion) for each phase, then analyse the difference. Add rows for more phases.*

- *Body position*
- *Angles of joints (knee, hip, shoulder, elbow, ankle)*
- *Release angle (throwing), flight (jumping or throwing), stride length and number (running)*
- *Speed/time and baseline score (for example time, distance or height)*
- *Principles such as balance, force, power, flow, fluency and coordination*
- *Strengths and weaknesses explained next to each screenshot*
- *Lines or angles drawn with an app (for example Kinovea, Hudl or Coach's Eye)*
- *PHE terminology (for example muscle names, flexion, extension)*

**Table columns in your document:** Phase of event, My client, Perfect model, Analysis of performance.

### RELEVANT SOLUTIONS AND NEXT STEPS

*Say what your client needs to work on and explain the solutions you will provide to make them better. Explain how the coach-client relationship, the environment and communication will affect their performance and lead to development.*

- *Specific areas/phases/components/skills/wellbeing elements that need improved, backed up by your results*
- *Link to relationships, environment, development and communication*
- *Explain how you will help your client improve and reach their goals (name specific training methods and exercises)*
- *How these will lead to improvement and why these solutions suit your client*
- *How you will observe progress (for example video analysis, questionnaires, feedback meetings, regular re-testing)*

### BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

- Written work is 5/6 pages maximum

- All text is 10pt or larger (including tables, screenshots and diagrams)
- Information is detailed and in paragraph form
- Tables, graphs, pictures and screenshots support your analysis
- PHE terminology is used throughout
- *words linking to the unit concepts, global context and statement of inquiry are in bold and/or different colour*
- Harvard citations sit next to the work they support (in-text citations) and save full sources for final bibliography
