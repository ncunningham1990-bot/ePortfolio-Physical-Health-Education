> **Unit title:** To be or not to be...online
> **Global context:** Identities and Relationships
> **Key concept:** Communication
> **Related concepts:** Environment; Interaction
> **Statement of inquiry:** Communication within a digital environment can influence how identity and relationships are formed.

## What you need to do

Observe and analyse your client's **health** and **physical performance** in your chosen Athletics event, then explain what they need to work on. The checklist below shows exactly what to include.

## Checklist: what to include

### Unit terminology and connections

1. Explain in detail how the e-Portfolio, Athletics, PHE, physical, mental and social well-being, and your chosen sport all relate to the **global context, key concept, related concepts and statement of inquiry**.
2. Use the group Padlet discussion to help you with ideas, along with online sources. **Make sure you reference AI and online sources correctly.**
3. See previous examples for help too.

### Health analysis

4. Write the interview questions you want to ask your client about their health, well-being and fitness. Also complete a **PAR-Q**.
5. Include other information such as average hours of sleep per day, steps per day, screen time, and any other relevant **health** information that could be a useful baseline.
6. Discuss how this was measured (for example ACSI, WHO well-being, CSAI-2, interviews, SMUIS, BSMAS, observation of performance).
7. **Results:** what does this information tell you? Display results in an appropriate format such as tables, graphs, screenshots or illustrations.
8. Relate your findings to the health, diet and sports psychology theory covered in lessons **and beyond**. For example: mental and social well-being, Inverted U Theory, motivation, somatic and cognitive anxiety, arousal, confidence, stress, pressure, diet nutrients, muscle building, sleep and recovery.
9. Analyse these answers and the results of the PAR-Q. Comment on the overall health, fitness and online device use of your client (age, height, weight, BMI, previous injury or illness, recent surgery, diet, calorie intake, water intake, sleep, recovery days, levels of activity, steps and screen time).
10. Refer to **innovative tools** you could use to monitor health, well-being and screen time. Starting points:
    - [20+ Fitness Tools that Track Your Exercise, Meals, Sleep, and More (zapier.com)](https://zapier.com/blog/best-fitness-tracking-apps/)
    - [Technology for Athletic Performance (perch.fit)](https://www.perch.fit/blog/technology-for-athletic-performance)
11. Also ask questions about:
    - relationships (for example coach, client, training partner)
    - how long they spend on devices and social media, and the impact the digital environment has on health and well-being. Use surveys to investigate this (Social Media Use Integration Scale, **SMUIS**, or Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale, **BSMAS**)
    - patterns of movement (understanding and development of skills, techniques and strategies)
    - the use of innovative tools (for example to monitor performance, health or current fitness)
    - self-improvement (for example intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation, anxiety, stress)
12. **Research and reference** to support and validate what you are saying.
13. Evaluate how these aspects and choices could affect the overall performance of your client in their chosen athletic event.

### Physical analysis

14. Introduce and explain the chosen Athletics event. **Reference your sources.**
15. Discuss how the **environment** in which you train and compete influences performance and physical skill (training facilities, relationship with the coach, the environment your coach creates, the digital environment and being online).
16. What components of fitness are needed for this event? Explain and define them.
17. What fitness tests have you conducted, and why?
18. Present the results of relevant baseline **fitness tests** and the **athletic event**. Use **3 trials and the average of each test**. Include a picture of your client performing these and display the results in an appropriate format (tables, graphs).
19. Investigate normative data, world records, national records, DASSA records and so on, and compare your client's results. Present your findings clearly in a table and highlight the range where your client's results fall. **Relate these results to social media influences.**
20. Record and analyse your client's technique in the athletic event. Think about:
    - body position
    - angles of joints (knee, hip, shoulder, elbow, ankle)
    - release angle (if throwing)
    - flight (if jumping or throwing)
    - stride length and stride number (running events)
    - speed of running (jumping and running events)
    - baseline score (time taken to complete the race, or distance of the throw or jump)
    - applying principles like balance, force and coordination

    As part of this analysis:
    - Insert screenshots and explain your client's strengths and weaknesses, comparing them to a "perfect model" (for example an Olympic athlete, world champion or professional).
    - Use PHE terminology (for example muscle names, and joint movements such as flexion and extension).
    - Refer to and use **innovative tools** for your analysis. Apps such as Kinemaster, Kinovea, Hudl and Coach's Eye let you draw angles, lines and circles on images to support what you are saying.

### Summary and next steps

21. What areas do they need to work on? How are the coach-client **relationship**, the **environment** and **communication** going to impact on their performance and athletic **identity**?
22. What will you now do to help them improve and achieve these goals? You will create measurable SMART goals to track progress and develop a performance development plan to support them in their journey.
23. How will you observe their performance and progress to see if they have improved throughout the rest of the ePortfolio? (For example video analysis, questionnaires, feedback meetings, re-testing.)
24. How is your feedback going to be provided? Use a digital environment, for example Padlet.
25. Relate **all of this back** to the MYP key concept, related concepts, global context and statement of inquiry. Put these words in bold or a different colour.
