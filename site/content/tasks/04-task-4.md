> **Unit title:** To be or not to be...online
> **Global context:** Identities and Relationships
> **Key concept:** Communication
> **Related concepts:** Environment; Interaction
> **Statement of inquiry:** Communication within a digital environment can influence how identity and relationships are formed.

## The task

Reflect on your ePortfolio. **Analyse and evaluate:**

- your interpersonal skills, **as coach and as client**
- the physical and health training plans, **as a coach**
- your **final performance** in relation to the performance development plan and the physical and health goals set in Task 2, **as a client**.

## Checklist: what to include

### As a coach

- Explain the strategies you used to enhance interpersonal skills when working with your client (see the skills examples at the bottom of this page).
- Explain, with examples, how you demonstrated strategies to enhance your interpersonal skills with your client.
- What strategies for collaboration with the client did you use, or could you use, to lead to ongoing adaptation and development of the training plan?
- What feedback did you give your client that led to changes in the plan? Give **examples**.
- What interpersonal and ATL skills did you use, or need to develop, to make this relationship work effectively? (**self-confidence, engagement, professional relationships, listening, attitude, collaboration, organisation, motivation, resilience, growth mindset**)
- What **IB learner profile attributes** have you developed (for example Risk-taker, Communicator, Principled)? Use examples to support this: [Learner profile for IB students (ibo.org)](https://www.ibo.org/benefits/learner-profile/)
- Analyse and evaluate the effectiveness of the training plans, including:
  - how the goals were met
  - a comparison to your client's initial performance results. Show in a table the results of your **client's initial, interim and final performances for both the athletic event and the well-being goals**
  - what you might do differently another time
  - the successes and challenges throughout.

Useful reading:
- [Nurturing Sport Expertise: Factors Influencing the Development of Elite Athlete (PMC, nih.gov)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3937568/)
- [Believe Perform: coaching articles](https://believeperform.com/category/coaching/)

### As a client

- Explain the strategies you used to enhance interpersonal skills when working with your coach.
- Explain, with examples, how you demonstrated strategies to enhance your interpersonal skills with your coach.
- Give examples of feedback you gave your coach that led to changes in the training plan.
- Analyse and evaluate your final performance (as shown in Task 3), including reference to the stated physical and health goals.
- What interpersonal and ATL skills did you use, or need to develop, to make this work? (**self-confidence, engagement, professional relationships, listening, attitude, collaboration, organisation, motivation, resilience, growth mindset**)
- What **IB learner profile attributes** have you developed (for example Risk-taker, Communicator, Principled)? Use examples to support this: [Learner profile for IB students (ibo.org)](https://www.ibo.org/benefits/learner-profile/)
- **Analyse your own final performance.** Explain whether there was an improvement or not, and identify why.
- Display all results of the **fitness tests, athletics event and health goal results** (questionnaires) in a table. Compare these to the initial and interim performance.
- Compare your performance to the SMART goals. Were they achieved?
- **Break the athletic event down into phases.** Compare your initial performance to your final performance. Use screenshots to show clearly the difference in technique between initial and final performance. Explain in detail the strengths and limitations of each phase.
