## Rubric: Criterion C, Applying and Performing

Your video is the evidence. Find the level that best matches it, then look at the level above to see how to improve.

| Level | What the criterion says, and what the video must show |
|---|---|
| **1-2** | **Criterion:** you demonstrate and apply skills and techniques with limited success, and demonstrate and apply strategies and movement concepts with limited success.<br><br>**Your video shows:**<br>• few and/or incorrect skills<br>• few and/or incorrect techniques<br>• strategies and patterns of movement that show limited understanding of the strategic use of space, time, force and flow of movement. |
| **3-4** | **Criterion:** you demonstrate and apply skills and techniques, and demonstrate and apply strategies and movement concepts.<br><br>**Your video shows:**<br>• basic skills<br>• basic technique<br>• basic strategies<br>• basic patterns of movement. |
| **5-6** | **Criterion:** you demonstrate and apply a **range** of skills and techniques, and a **range** of strategies and movement concepts.<br><br>**Your video shows:**<br>• a simple combination of basic skills<br>• intermediate technique<br>• a combination of basic strategies<br>• patterns of movement that show some strategic use of space, time, force and flow of movement. |
| **7-8** | **Criterion:** you demonstrate and apply a range of skills and techniques **effectively**, and a range of strategies and movement concepts **effectively**.<br><br>**Your video shows:**<br>• a combination of complex skills<br>• sophisticated technique<br>• a combination of complex strategies<br>• patterns of movement that show skilled strategic use of space, time, force and flow of movement. |

Not sure what command words like *demonstrate* or *analyse* mean? See the [command terms on the rubric page](#tasks/0).
