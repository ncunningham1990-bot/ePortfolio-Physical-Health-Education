## Rubric: Criterion B, Planning for Performance

Find the level that best matches your work, then look at the level above to see how to improve.

| Level | What the criterion says, and what it means in this task |
|---|---|
| **1-2** | **Criterion:** you **state** a plan for improving health and/or physical activity, and **state** the effectiveness of a plan.<br><br>**In this task:** you briefly state physical and/or health goals that are not specific and may not be achievable. Your plan is:<br>• incomplete<br>• illogical or unreasonable in its activities<br>• not flexible or responsive to the client's development<br>• lacking in detail, and may not lead towards physical and/or health goals. |
| **3-4** | **Criterion:** you **outline a basic** plan for improving health and/or physical activity, and **state** the effectiveness of a plan **based on the outcome**.<br><br>**In this task:** you briefly summarise physical and health goals that may be measurable, achievable, relevant or timely. Your performance development plan includes detailed information that:<br>• is logical<br>• refers to drills and skill technique(s)<br>• connects health and physical goals<br>• reflects some response to the client's development.<br><br>You partially break down the issues highlighted to create your plan and comment on the client's progress towards the physical and health goals, but rarely (if at all) adjust the plan. |
| **5-6** | **Criterion:** you **outline** a plan for improving health and/or physical activity, and **identify** the effectiveness of a plan **based on the outcome**.<br><br>**In this task:** you explain detailed physical and health goals that are specific, measurable, achievable and timely. Your detailed performance development plan:<br>• is clear<br>• demonstrates logical progression towards achieving goals<br>• includes drills that practise appropriate skill techniques<br>• develops a health element that connects with the physical (movement) goal(s)<br>• provides opportunity to reflect on progress and adapt the plan based on the client's development<br>• references supporting research (may include images, videos, data).<br><br>You break down the issues highlighted to create your plan and adapt the plan based on the client's progress towards the physical and health goals. |
| **7-8** | **Criterion:** you **construct and outline** a plan for improving health and/or physical activity, and **describe** the effectiveness of a plan **based on the outcome**.<br><br>**In this task:** you justify detailed physical and health goals that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and timely. Your detailed performance development plan:<br>• is specific, clear and concise<br>• demonstrates logical progression towards achieving goals<br>• includes drills that practise appropriate skill techniques<br>• develops a health element that connects with the physical (movement) goals<br>• dynamically adapts the plan in response to performance<br>• includes valid reasons for design choices<br>• references supporting research (may include images, videos, data).<br><br>You break down the issues highlighted to create your plan, and regularly adapt and justify changes to the plan based on the client's progress towards the physical and health goals in each training session. |

Not sure what command words like *justify* or *outline* mean? See the [command terms on the rubric page](#tasks/0).
