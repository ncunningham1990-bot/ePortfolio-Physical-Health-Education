## Checklist: what to include

### Factors affecting performance

- Discuss how the identified **weak physical aspects** (for example components of fitness such as *power, speed, strength*, and which aspects of the technique or phase of the event) are impacting performance in your chosen Athletics event.
- Discuss how the identified **weak health aspects** (for example lack of protein, lack of sleep, too much social media, lack of confidence, lack of motivation, fear of failure, not being able to perform in front of crowds, anxiety) are impacting performance in your chosen Athletics event.
- This is similar to the previous section in Task 1, so you can put this section together.

### Physical and health goals

- Identify challenging but obtainable goals.
- Develop meaningful goals towards improving performance that go beyond your client's physical development and include growth in physical, social, mental and/or spiritual well-being (Bi):
  - **one physical goal** that will improve your client's performance in the Athletics event
  - **one health goal** that focuses on psychology and will improve your client's performance. Relate it to social media use.
- Set goals in **SMART** format (*Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Timely*).
- Explain the relationship between your two goals and how improving them will lead to overall improvement in your Athletics event.

### Training methods

- Define and explain your chosen **physical training method(s)** (for example interval, circuit, weights, plyometric).
- Discuss **technique training**: give examples of specific drills for your Athletics event (**research these**). Explain which aspects of your client's technique need improving (for example take-off, body position) and give examples of exercises that can improve them.
- Define and explain your chosen **psychological skills training method(s)** (for example breathing techniques, visualisation, mental imagery, positive self-talk, muscle relaxation, meditation).
- Within the psychological skills training, explain the **changes to be made to your client's social media use and screen time**, and why and how these changes will affect performance and health.
- Explain why you chose these training methods and psychological skills, and how they will lead to improvements in your client's physical performance, health and well-being.

### FITT and SPORT principles

- Define and explain the principles of training (**SPORT** and **FITT**).
- Explain and give examples of how and why these will be applied to your training programme.

### Innovative tools to track and monitor performance

- Explain and give examples of how you will use innovative tools to track and monitor your client's performance.
- With reference to being "digital": how will you track your client's screen time and social media usage if this is an aspect of change?

### Training programme: sessions 1 to 4

- A structured training plan based on the goals identified.
- Both the health and the physical goals need to be planned for.
- Each session should include a **warm-up, main activity(ies) and cool-down**.
- Each session should also include **psychological skills training**.
- Show logical progression (FITT and SPORT) towards achieving the goals, including drills and activities that practise appropriate techniques and mental well-being.
- Use **innovative tools** to track performance (for example heart rate, workout duration, calories burned, stress levels after psychological skills training, relaxation or meditation apps, diet tracker, sleep tracker).

### Interim performance analysis

- **Re-test the fitness tests and athletic event** halfway through the programme (after 4 sessions) to measure progress.
- Report on progress towards your SMART goals and what changes need to be made to the initial training plan to help your client achieve them.
- Do you need to change your goal?

### Training programme: sessions 5 to 8

- A structured training plan based on the goals identified. **Adaptations should be evident based on the results of the interim performance, with clear examples of changes made from sessions 1-4 to sessions 5-8.**
- Each session should include a warm-up, main activity(ies) and cool-down.
- Each session should also include psychological skills training.
- Show logical progression (FITT and SPORT) towards achieving the goals, including drills and activities that practise appropriate skill techniques and mental well-being.

### Daily reflections

- Include a reflection after each training session on how your client is responding to the training and how your coach-client relationship is developing. Post reflections on your coach-client **Padlet** and take a screenshot as evidence. In the table on the template, summarise your client's reflections from the Padlet.
- Interview your client after each session and record their feedback (voice memo uploaded to Padlet): *What went well? What was difficult? What could be changed for next session?*
- You can give feedback by taking videos of your client during technique training and adding a voice-over. This creates the digital environment between you and your client.
- Report on the effect this programme is having on the health and physical goals.
- Insert screenshots of the **innovative apps** you have used to evidence your client's performance and progress.
- **Analyse** and evaluate progress towards the goals and make modifications to your plan, based on feedback from a discussion with, or observation of, the client.
- **Justify** the changes you will make, or have made, to your original plan.
- What changes do you need to make to your training programme (FITT and SPORT) based on these results?
