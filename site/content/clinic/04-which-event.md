Patient: Zara, 15, athletics newcomer
Intro: Zara wants to join the athletics club. A muscle biopsy at a sports science day showed she has more fast twitch fibres than average.

Scene: Which events would you suggest first?
Data: Fast twitch fibres: high proportion | Slow twitch fibres: lower proportion
+ Sprints and jumps, which need fast, powerful contractions | Fast twitch fibres contract quickly and forcefully, which suits short explosive events.
- The marathon, which needs lots of endurance | Endurance events are better matched to the other fibre type.
- Any event, because fibre type never matters | Fibre type is not the only factor, but it does influence which events suit a person.
- Only walking events | Walking is not a good match for her strengths.

Scene: Why might Zara find a long, steady run difficult?
+ Fast twitch fibres tire quickly, so they are less suited to long events | Right. Fast twitch fibres are powerful but fatigue faster than slow twitch fibres.
- Fast twitch fibres use oxygen more efficiently than anything else | They are better for short bursts, not efficient oxygen use over a long time.
- Fast twitch fibres cannot be trained at all | Fibres can be trained, though the proportion you have is largely inherited.
- Fast twitch fibres only work when you are asleep | Fibre type has nothing to do with sleep.

Scene: In the sprint start, Zara drives her front leg straight. Which muscle is the agonist for straightening the knee?
+ Quadriceps | Correct. They contract concentrically to extend the knee while the hamstrings relax as the antagonist.
- Hamstrings | The hamstrings bend the knee, so they are the antagonist here.
- Biceps | The biceps work at the elbow.
- Deltoid | The deltoid works at the shoulder.

Scene: Which training would best fit the principle of specificity for a sprinter?
+ Short sprint repeats with long rests and plyometric jumps | These train fast, explosive movements, which match sprinting.
- Steady two-hour jogs | That trains endurance, which is not what a sprint needs.
- Only stretching sessions | Flexibility helps, but it does not train speed or power on its own.
- Swimming lengths slowly | Slow swimming does not match the demands of sprinting.

Debrief: Zara's fibre make-up suggests power events, and her training should match the demands of the sprint, through specificity.
