Patient: Alex, 15, basketball player
Intro: Alex eats a takeaway most days, drinks lots of fizzy drinks and rarely eats fruit or vegetables. Alex wants more energy for basketball.

Scene: Looking at a typical day, what is the biggest problem?
Data: Breakfast: none | Lunch: burger and chips | Drinks: 3 fizzy drinks | Dinner: pizza
+ The diet is unbalanced: high in fat, sugar and salt and low in fibre, vitamins and minerals | A balanced diet includes all food groups in the right proportions, and this one lacks variety.
- It contains too many vegetables | The data shows very few vegetables, so look again.
- It contains too much water | The data shows fizzy drinks, not lots of water.
- It is perfectly balanced | A balanced diet needs variety, which is missing here.

Scene: Which swap for lunch gives the best improvement?
+ A wholegrain wrap with chicken and salad, plus fruit | This adds protein, carbohydrate, fibre and vitamins.
- Two burgers instead of one | This increases fat and salt even more.
- A sugary cereal bar | This is a small snack that does not fix the lack of variety.
- A large portion of fries | This adds more fat and little else.

Scene: Which drink choice is best for Alex to make at training?
+ Water, with a sports drink only for very long or intense sessions | Water hydrates without added sugar, and a sports drink is mostly useful for longer efforts.
- A large energy drink | Energy drinks often contain a lot of sugar and caffeine.
- Cola | This adds sugar and little else.
- No drink at all | Dehydration lowers performance and concentration.

Scene: Which snack is the smartest choice before practice?
+ A banana and a handful of nuts | The banana gives quick carbohydrate and the nuts add some healthy fat and protein.
- A bag of crisps | This is high in fat and salt with little else.
- A chocolate bar | This gives quick sugar but not much staying power.
- A fizzy drink | This is mostly sugar and water, so it does not support training.

Debrief: Small, realistic swaps toward fruit, vegetables, wholegrains and water improved balance and energy without a complete overhaul.
